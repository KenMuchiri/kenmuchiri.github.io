Notes From Building MshauriAI

June 2026

MshauriAI started as a Build with Gemma hackathon project — a personal and business financial advisor that reasons over someone’s actual financial context instead of giving the same generic advice to everyone who opens the app.

What a hackathon weekend actually teaches you has less to do with the technology and more to do with scope. We had roughly two days. Every hour spent on something that didn’t directly serve “can this give genuinely useful, context-aware financial advice” was an hour we didn’t have. That constraint turned out to be a gift — it forced a level of focus that’s much harder to hold onto with unlimited time.

MshauriAI ranked best in the hackathon, which was a genuinely good feeling. But the more useful outcome was smaller and more durable: proof, to myself as much as anyone, that a tightly scoped agentic system built on real context can outperform something broader and more generic. That lesson is now baked into how AlphaAI approaches every product we build.
