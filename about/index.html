About

Founder first.
Engineer by necessity.

I’m Ken Muchiri, founder and CEO of AlphaAI. I study economics and philosophy at the University of Nairobi while building practical technology for African realities.

The thread

From questions
to systems

Most technology companies start from the technology. I start from a market failure: the global AI wave has concentrated almost entirely in the US, Europe, and parts of Asia — trained on foreign data, priced for foreign markets, built on assumptions that don’t hold across most of Africa.

I founded AlphaAI in January 2023 to explore that gap — a Nairobi-based company building applied AI for African realities. Today AlphaAI has a founding team of three and four flagship AI products in build: Cloud Shule, HealthIQ, ClimasenseAI, and MshauriAI.

MshauriAI, an agentic financial advisor, was ranked best at the Build with Gemma hackathon — early proof that applied AI, built right, can genuinely compete.

How I Think

Three habits of thought

Economics

Technology only matters if it changes an incentive or an outcome for a real market. I ask what the market failure is before I ask what the model is.

Philosophy

Studying philosophy at the University of Nairobi keeps me honest about what a system should do, not just what it can do.

Practice

A good idea earns its place by meeting reality: users, constraints, trade-offs, and consequences.
